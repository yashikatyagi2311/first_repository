Introduction to Robotics

Robotics is the branch of engineering and science that involves the design, construction, operation, and use of robots.

  • Robots = mechanical + electronic + software integration
  • Used in manufacturing, healthcare, defense, and space exploration

Mechanical Components

  • Cams & Gears: Convert motion types
  • Bearings: Reduce friction in moving parts
  • Pneumatic/Hydraulic Systems: Use air or fluid pressure for actuation
  • Actuators: Motors or cylinders that drive movement

Control Systems

  • Sensors: Detect environment (IR, ultrasonic, vision)
  • Microcontrollers: Process inputs and control outputs
  • Feedback Loops: Maintain accuracy and stability
  • Programming: Defines robot behavior

Applications of Robotics

  • Industrial automation (assembly lines)
  • Medical robots (surgery, rehabilitation)
  • Military drones and defense systems
  • Exploration (Mars rovers, underwater robots)
